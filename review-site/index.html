    RECORDED FACTS → OUR CALCULATIONS

    An inspectable chain of evidence.

    Standard football definitions, our own adjustments and fitted weights.

    01

    Record the facts

    Scores, opponents, locations and recorded box-score counts, linked by exact game and team identities.

    02

    Rebuild the rates

    Calculate efficiency, conversions, turnovers and penalty-yard measures from their recorded counts and opportunities.

    03

    Account for opponents

    Estimate separate offensive and defensive allowance effects, with venue and FBS/FCS context. Sparse units retain visible evidence counts.

    04

    Learn the weights

    Older games teach the predictor which inputs help. Later tests assess those frozen choices; current team names never set their weights.

    THE FIRST VERSION

    Nine reconstructed measures.

    • Yards per recorded offensive play
    • Net passing yards per recorded pass attempt
    • Rushing yards per recorded rush attempt
    • Completion rate
    • First downs per recorded play
    • Third- and fourth-down conversion rates
    • Turnovers and penalty yards per recorded play

    Older validation selected the four efficiency measures alongside our existing Strength estimate. More available columns do not automatically earn a predictive weight.

    THE INPUT BOUNDARY

    Provider conclusions stay out.

    This candidate excludes provider PPA/EPA, success-rate aggregates, PPA-based explosiveness, team/player ratings, win probabilities, polls, recruiting stars and betting inputs.

    Missing raw information remains unavailable. An unobserved unit may receive an explicitly shrunken estimate; that does not create a fictional zero-valued game.

    Player, raw-play, drive and complete special-teams features still require audited inputs.

    DATA AND REPRODUCTION

    Keep the source visible.

    Recorded box-score counts come from retained ESPN data distributed by SportsDataverse. CFBD histories supply canonical game identities, results, subdivisions and timing. The current illustration uses the saved primary publication's completed game set.

    Our existing Performance view remains a separate benchmark using provider success-rate aggregates and our own adjustment. It is not relabeled as an independently rebuilt all-play measure.
