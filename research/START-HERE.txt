FBS INTELLIGENCE: RANKING EVALUATION

Purpose
Rank teams by what they earned from completed current-season games, accounting
for schedule difficulty. Keep forecasts separate from earned achievement.

Rules
No human polls as inputs or optimization targets. No team overrides or prestige
bonuses. Historical team strength supplies no current-season achievement credit.
General historical evidence may inform frozen probability and venue parameters.

Current decision
Keep production unchanged. Current Colley achievement is the benchmark.
Original-parameter, exact leave-one-game-out Strength of Record is a challenger.
Do not blend models or promote a challenger just because its order looks familiar.

What is saved
evaluation-standard.json: complete versioned evaluation rules and limitations.
results/weekly/<run fingerprint>/weekly-review.html: readable weekly replay report.
results/weekly/<run fingerprint>/weekly-rankings.csv: every ranked team/checkpoint.
results/weekly/<run fingerprint>/weekly-replay.json: diagnostics and input/code hashes.

How to reproduce (Python with NumPy; Node for production-equivalence test)
From the project folder:
  python research/test_research.py
  python research/replay_weekly.py

The replay uses stored 2021-2025 seasons. It writes only research/results.
No API credentials, network access, provider calls or new services are required.
Production code, snapshots, frontend and configuration are hashed before/after.

How to interpret results
Top-25 agreement, small movement and forecast success are not automatic measures
of achievement-ranking correctness. Check data completeness, underlying game
evidence, probability assumptions and uncertainty before proposing a replacement.
The 2021-2022 archive has no postseason. All replay years were previously inspected;
they cannot be relabeled as untouched tests. Genuine future evidence is still needed.

Research is local. These files do not schedule work or change the public rankings.
