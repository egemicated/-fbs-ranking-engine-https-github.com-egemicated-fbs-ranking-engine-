The protocol was written before fitting or viewing new later-year model results.
It fixes raw metric contracts, fitting2014-2017, selection2018-2019,
refitting2014-2019 and repeated retrospective evaluation2023-2025.
No current2026 ranking selected the model. Familiar evaluation data is not
described as an untouched or prospective trial.

After the first result, a reporting-only change expanded cached metric-support
metadata from union observed-game counts to separate offense/allowance counts.
A pure explained-prediction helper and portable research model artifact were
also added. The observation values, source/eligibility, candidate families,
selection grid, numerical fits, scoring and uncertainty method did not change.
Final metric values are checked against the first result before publication.

The selected broad candidate and control use calibrated venue-aware probability
picks. Their accuracy must not be directly compared to the previous blend
experiment's neutral higher-rank-winner percentage as if both were the same test.

This is the first bounded recorded-box-count candidate. It is not presented as
an all-CFBD, all-player, all-play or complete special-teams system. Additional
inputs require underlying recorded evidence, a declared population and a
separate chronological test. Standard statistical methods and football metric
definitions are reusable; independent implementation does not imply invention
of probability theory, ridge regression or familiar arithmetic ratios.

No raw source responses, normalized arrays, keys or credentials are published.
The current SOR, Strength, Performance and frozen future-blend experiment remain
unchanged. No source API calls, new services or paid resources were used.
