Decision
Research and validation are complete for the two declared candidates. The
revised candidate passes nine of ten engineering checks. Do not promote it to
the live website as an approved Performance ranking: one FBS-FCS bias gate
remains failed. SOR, Predictive Strength and the Sunday workflow are unchanged.

What was built
An opponent-adjusted net-success-rate model, with an exact explanation for every
score. A successful play gains at least50% of needed yards on first down,70% on
second and100% on third/fourth, following CFBD's documented scoring exceptions.
The observed game target is a team's offensive success rate minus its opponent's.
Each paired game has one weight. Teams retain individual identities, including
FCS opponents. There are no poll, odds, recruiting, conference-reputation,
win/loss, margin or previous-team-rating features in this Performance candidate.

This measures scrimmage consistency on provider-covered plays. It does not yet
measure complete football performance, special teams or the full cost of a
turnover/short field. CFBD gates these observations on non-null PPA. We consume
the deterministic success-rate field and play counts, not proprietary PPA values,
but do not claim that every box-score play is covered.

Research process
V1 learns general venue/subdivision coefficients on2014-2017, chooses ridge on
2018-2019, then tests2023-2025. Its fixed older FBS-FCS adjustment left2.804pp
of FBS-oriented underprediction, beyond the predeclared2pp target. V1 remains
recorded as failed; see ../advanced-performance/V1-RESULT.txt.

V2 was declared after those results. It allows current-season completed-game
evidence to update general venue/division context around the older priors.
No previous team rating carries over. Ridges and context-prior strength still
select solely on2018-2019;2023-2025 is explicitly a repeated retrospective check.
The previously uninspected success-rate fields for2021-2022 were fetched only
after declaring V2 and provide additional retrospective evidence. Outcomes and
later-corrected provider data are familiar, so no prospective/untouched claim.

All replay steps exclude the target and other same-week statistics. The full
parent schedule anchors each week's earliest cutoff, with a24h kickoff buffer.
Effects and context use the identical eligible past graph, including available
FCS-FCS games. The simple-rate comparator learns scale and contextual feature
weights on older data and selects its own shrinkage. It has one additional
global calibration coefficient. We compare whole calibrated models rather than
claiming a causal, isolated benefit of opponent adjustment.
