What the data audit changed

We added 34 missing games between known FCS opponents and corrected three 2022 scores using NCAA and school sources. Those score corrections preserve the winners. Earlier FCS playoff rounds were often already present under a provider “regular” label; a zero labeled-postseason count did not mean zero playoff games. We checked actual identities and results.

The repaired data matches all 253 NCAA tournament games in 2014–2019 and 2021–2025. With SWAC championships and Celebration Bowls checked separately, all 274 expected games in those categories outside pandemic season 2020 match. This does not certify every regular-season FCS or lower-division schedule. The 2020 spring season remains incomplete and outside training and pooled evaluation.

NCAA championship records contain some date and score errors; the independent primary-source audit documents school corroboration and exact matches. The three saved-score corrections are in the adjudication record. Raw source files stay in the local cache; the compact repair release and hashes permit offline restoration.

The detail tables show the chance an average FBS reference team would beat each opponent at that venue, with that game's result removed from the calculation. They are not forecasts of the listed team's own win probability. A more expected win supplies less SOR evidence. Each actual outcome is counted once; there is no second subdivision multiplier in the game network or record.

Limits that remain

  • The crossdivision evaluation sample has 595 FBS home games, seven neutral games and no FBS away games. This is not a causal neutral-field subdivision talent measure.
  • Some FCS regular-season and indirect lower-division context remains incomplete. Season2020 has missing roster identities and spring fixtures; no memberships were invented.
  • Sixty-seven unresolved provider field disagreements are FCS–FCS only: 36 neutral flags, 27 calendar labels, two kickoff times and two orientations. These do not change final-network Colley outcomes, but calendar choices can affect conservative preweek eligibility.
  • Better probabilities do not prove a uniquely correct achievement order. Current rank appearance was not a fitting target. No human polls or conference prestige bonuses were used.
  • Future crossdivision samples late in the season may be small. The model is not promoted automatically by agreement, a familiar Top25, or one week's forecast score.
