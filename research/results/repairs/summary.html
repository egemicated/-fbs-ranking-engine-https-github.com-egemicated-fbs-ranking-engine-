Safeguards activated; historical research repaired

The current rankings and predictive strengths are unchanged. Production received data-integrity safeguards. Historical coverage and venue experiments remain in a separate research dataset.

What is active

The weekly CFBD importer rejects ambiguous same-team, same-UTC-date completed games even when provider IDs or kickoff times differ. Conflicting scores and venue flags are recorded for investigation. A rejected refresh preserves the last publication. Historical CSV collisions are exposed in the audit rather than silently adjudicated.

Each future weekly run retains allowlisted provider responses, normalized inputs, a candidate snapshot and file checksums in a GitHub Actions artifact for 14 days. Failed normalizations retain current-attempt raw evidence where available. Authentication headers, environment files and credentials are excluded. No additional provider request is needed. The Sunday 08:00 America/Denver schedule is unchanged.

Commit afe5736a994e3eaf47c57440973f9dd3d1fded61 is live on Render. The home page, health endpoint and rankings endpoint all returned HTTP 200. All 138 ranking rows and predictive strengths match the prior publication; the home-page HTML also matches. The new artifact path was tested with mocked success and failure; no real provider refresh was triggered to test it.

Historical coverage repaired separately

A versioned research supplement adds 674 completed FBS postseason games across 2004–2022 and removes the eight previously confirmed duplicate occurrences. Every imported result has explicit season, completion, opponent identity, score and venue. No human polls, provider ratings, odds or supplied probabilities are imported.

Sources are cached monthly ESPN scoreboards and the public cfbfastR game-info archive. Team-oriented final scores for 593 added games were cross-checked between those sources. Four reversed home/away encodings were reconciled by team identity, not mistaken for contradictory scores. Two additional IDs for 2013 bowl games were excluded after a uniquely corroborated archive identity was found. No conflicting score was silently chosen.

The changes are small and mixed: four fewer correct winner picks, slightly better aggregate probability scores. These years were previously inspected, so this is retrospective evidence. Factual repairs are justified by completeness, not by selecting the dataset with the best accuracy.

In a current-season research scenario using the repaired historical prior, the maximum predictive-strength change is 0.005392 model points, with zero predictive rank changes. Current achievement and SOR do not use historical team credit, so this scenario does not alter them.

Venue findings

Using repaired 2014–2019 training history, the pregame probability fit estimates an equal-rated home team's chance at about 59.5%, compared with 61.6% under the original SOR coefficient. This is a general venue estimate, not a prediction for a named team.

On later prior-week-only retrospective observations, removing the original home adjustment worsens log loss in all three seasons. Refitting the adjustment helps in two seasons and worsens one:

This supports treating the contexts separately. Neither the refit nor the original coefficient has been promoted to a new production ranking.

What remains unresolved

  • Lower-division schedule and postseason completeness still varies by year.
  • The 2020 source roster omits three teams appearing as FBS in both adjacent years. No membership or record was invented.
  • Some 2004 duplicate variants disagree on venue. Identity repairs do not certify those venue fields.
  • The research supplement restores FBS postseason evidence; it is not certification of every game's metadata or all historical FBS membership.
  • The source-response artifacts begin with the next real weekly run. The old current raw CFBD response is not recreated by this release.
  • All historical experiments are retrospective. A frozen candidate still needs genuinely future evaluation.

Decision

Keep current ranking formulas. Keep the repaired dataset separate while auditing remaining coverage and venue issues. Use the new weekly evidence to investigate future data problems without additional CFBD requests. Do not blend achievement with predictive strength or select a coefficient based on familiar team positions.
