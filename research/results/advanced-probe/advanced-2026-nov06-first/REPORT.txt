What this does not establish
The earlier net-YPP prototype's subdivision bias has not been recalibrated or
retested using these metrics. Complete game coverage alone cannot fix a formula.
Historical-year coverage also remains to be checked before older-only fitting.

The provider aggregates plays where PPA is non-null. Disabling garbage-time
filtering therefore includes all its PPA-eligible game states, not every box-score
scrimmage play. Upstream aggregate nulls can become zero during serialization;
finite JSON numbers do not prove a valid denominator for every conditional rate.
The endpoint does not identify the PPA model version, training dates or complete
feature provenance. These need auditing before claiming a fully independent
performance measure or chronological forecast validation. Special teams,
turnovers, short fields and overtime still need explicit accounting policies.
