What original means here
Recorded game facts may come from a data provider. Standard football definitions
and public statistical methods are reusable. Our feature calculations, opponent
adjustments, fitted coefficients and predictions are inspectable and reproducible.
No CFBD PPA/EPA, provider success flags, PPA-based explosiveness, team/player
ratings, win probabilities, polls, recruiting stars or betting inputs enter
this candidate. Current Performance remains a disclosed provider-aggregate
benchmark; it is not relabeled as an independently rebuilt all-play statistic.

Current boundary
This first candidate uses the nine reliable count-based feature contracts in
the retained box archive. It does not invent raw-play success, explosive-play
frequency, drive finishing, player availability/contribution or complete
special-teams grades. Those extensions need audited recorded inputs, matching
opportunity populations, timing and their own declared tests. More fields are
not an automatic improvement and cannot be selected by familiar rankings.
