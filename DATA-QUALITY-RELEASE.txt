DATA QUALITY SAFEGUARDS

Ranking formulas, the dashboard, and the committed current/prior snapshots are unchanged.

Weekly CFBD imports now stop when completed records contain the same pair of
teams on the same UTC date under multiple IDs or kickoff times. The previous
publication remains available. Conflicting scores or venue flags are recorded
for review; the importer does not silently choose an occurrence.

Historical CSV imports expose those collisions in source.audit.logicalDuplicates.
They retain the original records until a versioned corrected dataset is selected.
The historical-coverage-v1 repair is research only and is not activated here.

The weekly workflow saves provider responses, normalized input, a candidate
snapshot, and file checksums as a GitHub Actions artifact for 14 days. Rejected
imports retain current-attempt raw responses and a structured rejection audit
when available. Environment files, authentication headers and credentials are
never copied. No additional CFBD requests are made for evidence retention.

The Sunday 08:00 America/Denver schedule and the two-call successful refresh
remain the same. This release does not trigger a manual provider refresh.

Validation: 29 Node checks, including mocked successful/rejected refreshes,
exactly-two-request checks, byte-preservation of the prior publication on
rejection, evidence checksums, and exclusion of credentials/stale evidence.
